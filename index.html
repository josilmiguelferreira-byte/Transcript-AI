Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the transcription? +
AssemblyAI uses Universal-2, one of the most accurate speech-to-text models available, reaching 95–99% accuracy on clear audio. It handles accents, background noise, and multiple speakers reliably.
What is word-by-word transcription? +
Word-by-word mode shows every single word with its exact timestamp (e.g. [00:03] Hello [00:04] everyone). This is ideal for subtitles, video editing, or finding specific moments in long recordings.
What file formats are supported? +
MP3, MP4, WAV, M4A, WEBM, OGG, FLAC, and most other audio and video formats. Files up to 5GB are supported.
Is my audio data secure? +
Yes. Your files are encrypted in transit and processed on secure AssemblyAI servers. Audio files are automatically deleted after transcription is complete.
How do I unlock Pro features? +
After purchasing a Pro plan, you will receive a unique access code via email. Enter this code in the Pro Unlock screen (click "Unlock Pro" in the Pricing page) to activate all Pro features.

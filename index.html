Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the transcription? +
Whisper Large v3 achieves 98–99% accuracy on clear audio. Background noise or strong accents may reduce accuracy. Always choose the largest model for best results.
Do I need an API key? +
Yes. You need a free Hugging Face API key. Create an account at huggingface.co, go to Settings → Access Tokens, and create a Read token. It is completely free.
What file formats are supported? +
MP3, MP4, WAV, M4A, WEBM, OGG, FLAC and more. Both audio and video files work. The system automatically extracts audio from video files.
Is my data secure? +
Your files are sent directly to Hugging Face servers for processing. They are not stored on our servers. Hugging Face automatically deletes files after processing.
What if transcription takes too long? +
Hugging Face's free tier may sometimes have queues. Try a smaller model (Whisper Base) or a shorter audio clip. Large files can take 1–3 minutes to process.

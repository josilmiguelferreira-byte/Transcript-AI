Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I reach my free limit? +
Once you've used your 20 free minutes, you'll need to upgrade to Pro ($12/month) for unlimited transcription. Your existing transcripts remain accessible.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Yes! All paid plans are billed monthly and you can cancel anytime. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.
What's the difference between Pro and Business? +
Pro is for individual power users with unlimited transcription. Business adds 5 team seats, API access, custom vocabulary, and priority support for teams.
Do you offer annual billing? +
Yes! Choose annual billing to get 2 months free. Pro becomes $120/year (save $24) and Business becomes $490/year (save $98).
Is my data secure? +
Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption, never store your credit card details (handled by Stripe), and automatically delete uploaded files after processing. We're GDPR compliant and SOC 2 certified.

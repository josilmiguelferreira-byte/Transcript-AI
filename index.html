Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I reach my free limit?+
Once you've used your 20 free minutes, you'll need to upgrade to Pro (€12/month) for 3000 minutes/month, or Business (€49/month) for unlimited transcription. Your existing transcripts stay accessible.
What's included in the Pro plan?+
Pro gives you 3000 minutes (50 hours) of transcription per month. Minutes reset on your billing date. This is more than enough for most podcasters, journalists, or researchers — that's roughly 100+ 30-minute interviews per month.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?+
Yes! All paid plans are billed monthly and you can cancel anytime through your account dashboard. You'll keep Pro or Business access until the end of your billing period with no additional charges.
What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), iDEAL (Dutch bank transfer), Google Pay, and Klarna (pay later). All payments are securely processed by Stripe.
What's the difference between Pro and Business?+
Pro is for individual users with 3000 min/month. Business gives your entire team unlimited transcription, priority processing, advanced analytics, and dedicated support — ideal for agencies and research teams.
Is my data secure?+
Absolutely. All uploads use SSL encryption. Files are automatically deleted after processing. We never store your payment details — that's handled securely by Stripe. We are GDPR compliant.
What audio/video formats are supported?+
We support MP3, MP4, WAV, M4A, WEBM, OGG, FLAC, AAC, and most other common audio and video formats. Free plan: up to 25 MB. Pro: up to 1 GB. Business: up to 2.2 GB per file.
Do you have a free trial?+
Yes! Every new account gets 20 free minutes with no credit card required. Pro and Business plans also include a 7-day free trial.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does auto language detection work? +
When you select "Auto-detect", AssemblyAI listens to the first 15–90 seconds of your audio and automatically identifies the spoken language with a confidence score. It supports all 99 languages. The detected language is shown after transcription.
How accurate is the transcription? +
AssemblyAI achieves 95–99% accuracy on clear audio. It handles accents, background noise, and multiple speakers reliably across all supported languages including Dutch.
What is word-by-word transcription? +
Word-by-word mode shows every single word with its exact timestamp (e.g. [00:03] Hello [00:04] everyone). This is ideal for subtitles, video editing, or finding specific moments in long recordings.
What file formats are supported? +
MP3, MP4, WAV, M4A, WEBM, OGG, FLAC and most other audio and video formats up to 5GB.
